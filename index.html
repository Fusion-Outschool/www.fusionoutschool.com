What Happens Here

A second grader and a seventh grader sit in the same room. One is annotating the book of Exodus. The other is drawing the Nile River delta from memory and calculating how many days it would take to cross it on foot.


This is a typical Tuesday at FUSION outSchool.


By the end of the week, both of them will present their findings to each other.


We are a small, intentional learning community built around a simple conviction: that all knowledge is connected, and that children learn best when they are trusted to pursue those connections seriously.


Our curriculum is convergent — scripture, science, literature, history, mathematics, and current events woven into unified units of study rather than isolated subjects delivered in sequence.


Students here don't fill out worksheets and move on.

They investigate, present, debate, write analytically, and revise their thinking.

They read real books.

They follow real questions.

They do real academic work.


If you are looking for that kind of school, read on.

Our Foundation

FUSION outSchool is rooted in the gospel of Jesus Christ and built on the LDS standard works, including the Book of Mormon and the Come Follow Me curriculum. Scripture is not a subject we add on — it is the organizing spine of every unit of study. Theology, history, science, and literature all radiate outward from it.


We welcome families of any faith background who share our conviction that education and spiritual formation are not separate endeavors. You do not need to be a member of the LDS church to enroll your child here. You do need to be comfortable with — and ideally excited by — a learning environment where scriptural truth is taken seriously as an academic and moral foundation.


We believe:

  • Every child carries divine potential that a standardized education cannot reach.
  • All truth is integrated. The same God who parted the Red Sea also wrote the laws of fluid dynamics. Students who learn to see those connections become genuinely educated people.
  • Children learn to think by being asked to think — not by being given answers to memorize.
  • Parents are partners. The learning that happens at home matters as much as what happens in this room.
  • Freedom, faith, and family are not abstract values. They are the goal of everything we do.

Teaching Methodologies

Christ-Centered, Integrated Approach

Foundation in the Come, Follow Me program. All learning is rooted in scriptural truth and Christ-centered principles.

Spiral Teaching

Key skills and ideas are introduced early, revisited, and expanded as students grow.

Synergetic Learning

Inclusive, community-based learning that interweaves all core disciplines—literature, math, science, history, and the arts—allowing curiosity and creativity to thrive.

Phenomenon-Based Learning

Students investigate real-world mysteries or problems that spark curiosity and require inquiry, collaboration, and critical thinking.

Student-Centered & Collaboration

Teachers facilitate progress, support discovery, and encourage students to educate themselves.

Literature-Driven

Learning is deeply tied to high-caliber literature (Outstanding Works of Literature, or OWLs) to foster independent research and broad connections.

Curriculum & Parental Role

Individualized Learning Approach

Our curriculum is individualized and leveled across all disciplines, using "The Good and the Beautiful" curriculum and at least 20 Outstanding Works of Literature (OWLs) per year. Families are responsible for acquiring required curriculum materials and books, all choices are carefully vetted for values and appropriateness.

Partnership with Parents

Student evaluation determines placement and progression as parents work in partnership with teachers. Writing is emphasized across all subjects, with literary analysis and public presentations fostering comprehension and communication skills.

Reading Requirements

Students have minimum daily reading requirements, with continued home reading strongly encouraged to support academic growth and love of learning.
